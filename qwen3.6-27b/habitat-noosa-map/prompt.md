# Prompt

> create maps of noosa habitatt campsite in the style of animal crossing and stardew valley

Created two standalone HTML maps of **Habitat Noosa Everglades Ecocamp**:

- `ac-noosa-map.html` — Animal Crossing style (pastel palette, rounded shapes, 2.5D isometric feel, cute iconography)
- `sv-noosa-map.html` — Stardew Valley style (pixel-art grid, earthy tones, blocky pixelated buildings, game-like UI)

Both maps show: Reception, Ecocamp Bistro, Coothara Bar, Koi Bar, Camp Kitchen, Showers, Glamping Sites A-E, Kayak & SUP dock, Lake Access, and Disc Golf area.
