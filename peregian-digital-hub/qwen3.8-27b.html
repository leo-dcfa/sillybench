Tokenizer · the AI training program

Surfing the AI wave on the Sunshine Coast

Tokenizer is Noosa Council's multi-year program to help the region develop the skills, knowledge and practical experience needed to thrive in the AI era — building a thriving AI talent base and an AI-literate community through hands-on learning and collaboration.

Supported by Advance Queensland, Regional Development Australia (Moreton Bay, Sunshine Coast & Noosa), Noosa Shire Council and Bendigo Bank — and delivered with Reboot Noosa, Coding from Beach, the Screen Collective, the Manufacturing Excellence Forum, Tourism Noosa and the Food & Agribusiness Network.

Our story

From a former bowls club to a home for Noosa's digital sector

How a struggling Peregian Beach Bowls Club became the hub for the region's founders, AI builders and creative technologists.

2013

The idea takes shape

A Council local economic strategy review flagged an over-reliance on tourism and a youth brain drain. A small digital sector — led by cloud company Atmail — had begun to form organically in Peregian Beach, along with the Silicon Coast and Coding from Beach meetups.

2017

Construction begins

With the Peregian Beach Bowls Club in receivership, Council acquired the site and, following community consultation, planned a digital hub on a third of it. Built by Hutchinson Builders for $3M — funded by ratepayers and a $1M state grant.

2018

The Hub opens

Officially opened on 7 July 2018 with Atmail as anchor tenant and an initial cohort of 20 full-time members — technologists from Levo, Even Labs, Creative Applause, Drumbeat Digital and Hey Piggy Bank.

2019–20

Fire, pandemic & PPE

2019's bushfires nearly destroyed the Hub and the township, saved by over 300 volunteer firefighters — inspiring the Firetech Connect program. During the 2020 pandemic the Hub was transformed into a facility producing PPE for frontline medical staff.

2023

The AI Lab is built

With federal bushfire-recovery and Building Better Regions funding, a world-class AI Lab was built — renovating the old Peregian Beach Community House and adding new facilities — opening in July 2023 to double as a Firetech trial facility.
