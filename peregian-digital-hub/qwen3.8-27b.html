01 The Hub

Part studio.Part club.All community.

One floor up from the beach, two floors from the highway. We built the room we wished existed on this coast — proper desks, honest coffee, a studio that works, and a door that stays open late. This is what it's for:

Come say hi
01
The Workroom

48 desks, zero drama

10G fibre, standing desks, a wall of light, and the quietest corner on the coast. Most members never leave before the tide does.

02
Studio B

Pods for sound & screen

Two treated pods — one video, one audio — with kit that's actually maintained. Podcasts, reels, client calls that need to sound like money.

03
Brew Hall

Coffee before ambition

The roaster upstairs, the bar downstairs, the queue at 8:05 sharp. Top-up credits, a proper kitchen, and toast that converts skeptics weekly.

04
The Yard

Where the week ends

Shaded strings, long tables, a speaker that takes abuse. Suppers, markets, mixers and the odd wedding. Hires out from $350, rain plan included.

06 Around the Hub

Two minuteson foot.

The postcode that works for us. Drag, scroll or just wander — every one of these is a lunch away.

Peregian Beach Park

2 min walk

The main stretch. Long boardwalk, the best light for a late lunch, and a beach bar that takes reservations now.

Creek Boardwalk

5 min walk

Where the morning walkers and the paddle boardsers collide. Go early for the calm, stay late for the colour.

The Fish Market

8 min walk

Saturday only. Catch of the day, a very opinionated fishmonger, and lunch that ends the debate.

Beach Bar

3 min walk

Umbrellas, a strong cocktail list, and the best seat in town if you claim it by ten. We mean it.

Espresso on the Coast

4 min walk

Not our bar, but we say it with a straight face. The flat white here is a benchmark we still lose to.
