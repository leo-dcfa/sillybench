Our story

From bowls club to beachside tech hub.

Noosa Shire Council built the Hub on the site of the old Peregian Beach Bowls Club to grow the region's digital economy and give local talent a reason to stay.

Read the full history →
NOV 2014

The site

Noosa Shire Council purchases the former Peregian Beach Bowls Club grounds on Rufous Street.

EARLY 2018

Built

Designed by Sparks Architects and built by Hutchinson Builders. The roughly $3 million project was funded by Council with a $1 million Queensland Government grant.

SEP 2018

Doors open

The Hub opens and starts delivering programs, events and activities for the region's tech entrepreneurs.

2019

Through the fire

Catastrophic bushfires nearly destroy the Hub and the Peregian Beach township. More than 300 volunteer firefighters save them.

2020

FireTech Connect

Out of that experience, the Hub launches a program to accelerate bushfire-resilience technology.

TODAY

AI Lab & Tokenizer

The Hub becomes an AI Lab and builder community, home to one of Australia's leading AI skills programs for founders.
