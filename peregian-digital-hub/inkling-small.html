The Peregian Digital Hub is a community-focused workspace designed for locals, remote workers, and visitors who need more than just an internet connection. Located in Peregian Beach on Queensland’s Sunshine Coast, we blend professional amenities with a welcoming, modern environment.

Whether you need a quiet desk for an afternoon, a meeting room for your team, or a space to host an event, the Hub offers reliable technology and comfortable surroundings.
