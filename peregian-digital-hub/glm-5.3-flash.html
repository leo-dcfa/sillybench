Why the Hub exists

Start with what you want to grow.

AI is reducing the time, cost and specialist expertise needed to create with technology. More people can now turn what they know into a tool, a product, a better way of working, or a new business.

The Hub exists to help local people and businesses take part. Some come to learn how to use new tools well. Others come to make tools of their own. Both need practical programs, useful people around them, and room to test an idea.

Our story

How a former bowls club became a home for Noosa’s digital sector.

2013

An idea takes root

Council’s economic strategy review flags over-reliance on tourism and a youth brain drain. The Peregian Beach Bowls Club goes into receivership; Council acquires the land and plans a knowledge campus on one third of it. Atmail, Silicon Coast and Coding from Beach show a digital sector is already forming.

2017 – 2018

The Hub opens

Construction begins in January 2017 — a $3 million build by Hutchinson Builders, funded by ratepayers and a $1m state grant. The Hub opens in July 2018 with Atmail as anchor tenant and an initial cohort of 20 full-time members.

2019 – 2020

Fire, pandemic — and AI cadets

Bushfires nearly take the Hub and township; 300+ volunteer firefighters save the day, inspiring the Firetech Connect program. In 2019 — three years before ChatGPT — the Hub launches its AI Cadetship. During COVID the Hub pivots to manufacturing PPE for frontline hospital staff.

2023

The AI Lab era begins

Federal grants fund a stage-2 expansion — a renovation of the old community house plus new construction by Carfax. The world-class AI Lab opens in July 2023 with a 100-seat auditorium and production studios.

Today

Tokenizer

A full-court press on AI upskilling for the whole region — helping startups like Springboards.ai and Nextplay emerge, and supporting SMEs, kids, teens, creatives and business leaders to bring their ideas to life with AI.
