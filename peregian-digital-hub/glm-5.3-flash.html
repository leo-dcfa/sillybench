01 The Hub

We took out the pews and put in fibre.

Peregian Digital Hub has called the old church on David Low Way home since 2017 — long timber tables where the pews used to be, leadlight windows, and a courtyard for the long lunches. Designers, developers, founders and remote teams all work under one set of rafters.

The dress code is barefoot-optional, the coffee is serious, and when you need to think, the ocean is a two-minute walk. The Hub is also part of the Peregian "smart town" story — free gigabit Wi-Fi across the whole village, a first for Australia.
