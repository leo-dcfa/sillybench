Our story

From a bowls club to a hub for AI builders.

2013

The idea takes root

Noosa's economic strategy identifies an over-reliance on tourism and a youth brain drain, while a small digital community forms around Peregian Beach. When the Peregian Beach Bowls Club goes into receivership, Council acquires the land and decides to create a knowledge campus on a third of the site.

2017–2018

The Hub opens

Construction begins in January 2017 — a $3 million build funded by ratepayers and a $1m state government grant. The Hub commences operations on 7 July 2018 with an initial cohort of 20 full-time members.

2019–2020

Firetech & resilience

After the bushfires that threatened Peregian Beach, the Hub launches Firetech Connect to accelerate bushfire-resilience technologies — and even becomes a PPE manufacturing facility during the pandemic.

2023

The AI Lab

A renovation and new build — delivered with federal bushfire recovery and Building Better Regions funding — adds a world-class AI Lab, officially opened in July 2023.

Today

A vibrant AI ecosystem

AI cadetships, residencies, hackathons, meetups, incubated startups and Uplift cohorts of local business leaders — a culture of learning, sharing, building and experimenting with AI.
