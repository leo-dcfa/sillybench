Our story

How a former bowls club became a home for Noosa's digital sector

The idea of developing the digital sector in the Noosa region gained traction after a 2013 Council Local Economic Strategy review identified an over-reliance on tourism and hospitality, a youth brain drain, and the need for more resilient high-value employment.

2013

The spark

The Peregian Beach Bowls Club went into receivership; Council acquired the land. Meanwhile Atmail, a cloud email provider, had established itself in Peregian Beach, and networks such as Silicon Coast and Coding from Beach began to form. A TEDx event in Noosa led by Brian Keayes put technology and innovation front and centre.

2017

Construction begins

Construction commenced in January 2017, funded through ratepayer funds plus a $1m grant from the state government, at a total cost of $3 million. It was completed by Hutchinson Builders in early 2018 after a 50-week build.

2018

The Hub opens

Officially opened by Qld Minister Stirling Hinchliffe, Mayor Tony Wellington and local member Sandy Bolton. Operations began on 7 July 2018 with Atmail as anchor tenant, alongside an initial cohort of 20 full-time members including technologists from Levo, Even Labs, Creative Applause, Drumbeat Digital and Hey Piggy Bank.

2019–2020

Firetech and pandemic response

The catastrophic 2019 bushfires almost burnt down the Hub and the entire township, saved by the brave efforts of over 300 volunteer firefighters. The experience inspired the Firetech Connect project. During the early pandemic, the Hub was transformed into a manufacturing facility producing PPE for frontline medical staff at Sunshine Coast hospitals.

2022–2023

Building the AI Lab

Federal bushfire recovery funding plus a Building Better Regions grant allowed Council to create the AI Lab — renovating the old Peregian Beach Community House and building new facilities. Carfax Commercial Constructions started work in December 2022; the AI Lab was completed in June 2023 and officially opened in July.

Today

A thriving AI ecosystem

A vibrant ecosystem of AI talent, startups and expertise, with a culture of learning, sharing and experimentation — and the Tokenizer program expanding support across the whole region.
