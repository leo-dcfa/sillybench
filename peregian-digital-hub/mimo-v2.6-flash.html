Apply for Membership

Am I locked in?

No. We don't collect payment details when you apply, and you can withdraw your application at any time.

How does billing work?

Memberships are billed monthly, in advance, via credit card. T&Cs are shown during sign-up.

How are applications assessed?

On tech industry fit, contribution to fellow members and talent programs, and your plans for growth — it's a curated space.

Learn · Tokenizer

Surfing the AI wave on the Sunshine Coast.

Tokenizer is a multi-year program from Noosa Council's Peregian Digital Hub to help this region build the skills, knowledge and practical experience needed to thrive in the AI era — for hub members, startups, SME leaders, storytellers, technologists, teenagers and kids. It's made possible through Advance Queensland's Regional Enabler Program, Regional Development Australia's Moreton Bay–Sunshine Coast–Noosa network, and Noosa Shire Council.

Uplift · Cohort 3

AI for SMB Leaders

A four-week AI adoption program for Sunshine Coast business owners and senior leaders. Starts 8 October 2026 — four Thursday mornings (8, 15, 22 & 29 October). $293 per business, up to 3 participants. Cohorts 1 & 2 reached 80 leaders from 53 local businesses. Subsidised 1:1 mentoring and proof-of-concept support available by EOI.
